# e-clearing.net seed notes (retrieved 2026-10-24)

Public technical material is thin. Most of this note is Unknown by design; do not fill the gaps from memory.

## Facts from the company site

- e-clearing.net GmbH, Krefelder Str. 195, 52070 **Aachen, Germany**. (Codibly's overview places it in the Netherlands and calls it a non-profit; the company's own site does not support either. The Dutch link is that the foundation ElaadNL co-initiated the platform.)
- Services: Roaming; Charge Club (publish, review and accept roaming offers online); NAP Access (one integration to several National Access Points).
- Roles served: CPOs, EMPs, NSPs, PSOs.
- Claimed scale: 25+ million tokens, 620,000+ charge points, 30+ countries, 1,800+ partners.
- Customer portal at `support.e-clearing.net/portal` (not reachable during the first run; do not try to log in).

## Facts from press (dated)

- Initiated by Smartlab (Ladenetz.de) and ElaadNL. A GmbH has coordinated the platform since August 2020 (electrive, 2020-12-20).
- Protocols: OCHP and OCPI. In late 2020 the new system eCHS 2.0 was in public alpha for both, with OCHP 1.5 and OCPI 2.2 planned the quarter after launch.
- Hubject and Smartlab announced a Plug&Charge cooperation in September 2021 (business level only): Smartlab to offer Plug&Charge to German municipal utilities with contract certificates; no technical interconnection described.
